Hi Wanters,

Wantd is officially live on the App Store today, and I wanted to send this to you personally because you were one of the very first people to sign up before the app even existed.

I’ve been building Wantd on my own, so seeing actual people join the waitlist, follow along and believe in the idea before there was even an app to download genuinely meant a lot to me.

The whole idea behind Wantd is pretty simple. I wanted one place for all the things we want and somehow keep scattered everywhere. Screenshots in our camera roll, 40 tabs left open, saved posts, things sitting in carts that we’re not ready to buy yet.

Instead, they can all live in one beautiful wishlist.
Every want you save, one beautiful wishlist.
And when your birthday, Christmas or any other occasion comes around, the people close to you can actually see what you want, secretly reserve something so nobody buys the same gift twice, or contribute together towards something bigger.

Wantd is completely free and invitation only.

Privacy Policy

Wantd is a link-based gift wishlist app. We designed it to collect the smallest possible amount of personal data.

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what we store, where we store it, and your rights.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

  • Your account. Wantd needs one way to recognise you: your Apple account, your Google account, or your email address. We keep an identifier of that account (never your password) and, if you came in by email or with a Google account that shares its address, your email address.
  • Profile. Your display name, optional profile photo, optional birthday, and a handle used to visit your profile by link.
  • Wishlist items. The product links you save, the images and text read from those pages, your personal notes, list names, and reservation/group-gift interactions.
  • What you write to us. Messages sent from "Write to us" and reports on a piece, with the version of the app.

We do NOT collect: your phone number, precise location, contacts, browsing history outside the app, or advertising identifiers. Wantd has no analytics and no ad tracking.

2. WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES

Your profile, your wishlist and your messages are stored in Wantd's database, hosted by Apple (iCloud/CloudKit) and operated by Sedona Tech. On Android and on the web, the app reaches that database through a server run by Sedona Tech on Cloudflare. That server also keeps the link between your account (Apple, Google or email) and your Wantd profile, and your email address when you gave one. The Wantd team can access this data to run the service, and for nothing else.

Signing in with Apple or Google happens on Apple's or Google's own pages: Wantd never sees your password. A sign-in code by email is sent through Resend, an email delivery service, and expires after ten minutes.

3. WHAT IS PUBLIC

Your profile and your wishlist are visible to people who know your handle. Pieces you keep public can also appear in the Feed, where Wantd members discover what others want, with your name and handle. Pieces filed on a private list are shown only to the members of that list. There is no search engine indexing.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Wantd uses a small number of services, strictly to make the app work:

  • Apple (iCloud/CloudKit) stores the database; Cloudflare hosts Wantd's server; Resend sends sign-in codes.
  • The product page you paste is fetched from your phone to read its image, title and price, and read again about once a day to keep the price fresh. The shop sees your phone's connection, as it would for a visit in your browser. Wantd does not track your browsing.
  • Store logos are loaded through Wantd's server (Brandfetch and logo.dev). A shop name you type is completed by Clearbit. Exchange rates come from a public rates service. A TikTok link is read through TikTok's embed service.
  • Shop links may go through Awin, an affiliate network: Wantd may earn a commission on what you buy, at no cost to you. Awin may set a cookie in your browser.
  • Google Play may invite you to rate Wantd, through Google's in-app review.

Price-drop notifications are made on your phone. Wantd uses no push service.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You can, at any time:

  • Edit or delete any piece of your wishlist, and edit your profile.
  • Delete your account from inside the app: your wishlist, your lists, your profile, your sign-in links and your email address are removed from Wantd for good.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or anything else, at support@sedonatech.uk.

6. CHILDREN

Wantd is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

7. CHANGES

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly, and material changes will be announced in the app.

8. CONTACT

Sedona Tech Ltd, support@sedonatech.uk

Delete your account

In the app: Profile › Edit profile › Delete my account. Your wishlist, your lists, your profile, your sign-in links and your email address are removed from Wantd for good, right away.

Without the app: write to support@sedonatech.uk from the address or the account you use with Wantd, and we delete it for you within a few days.
